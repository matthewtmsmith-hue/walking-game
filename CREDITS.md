## Living-room graphics upgrade (`assets/gfx/living/`)
All of these assets are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and are **CC0** (public domain, no attribution required; credited anyway).
They were converted with gltf-transform: meshopt geometry compression, WebP textures, models resized to max 2048 px (small props 1024 px), and the book set and big plant simplified to about 30% of their triangles. The materials were converted to WebP at 2048 px.
